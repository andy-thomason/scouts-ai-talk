# Scouts AI talk — "You Are the AI"

A talk for teenagers (scouts) on the basics of how LLMs work. Every scout plays a part of the LLM; an animated slideshow sets up each part.

Deck (Claude Slides artifact): https://claude.ai/artifact/75Au5vHbDepAQ5t8UftLwY

## Brief (from Amy, 2026-10-30)

1. **Next-word guessing** — an LLM just guesses the next word. Game: each scout in turn says one word of a story, starting from the prompt "Once upon a time".
2. **Word homes (embeddings)** — show how words get positions, with similar words close together. Do NOT say "2D", "vector" or "map".
3. **Attention** — "What is the capital of France?": countries and capitals in a word jumble with lots of other words. Every capital is the same direction from its country; that direction is the attention.
4. **Applications** — simple queries like "Where can I buy a loaf of bread?". The LLM looks up extra info (Wikipedia, the internet, your location) to build a story that answers the question.
5. **Agents** — doing things like booking trains or sending emails.
6. **Diffusion** — noise turning into a cat on a fence.
7. **Humanoid robots and jobs** — everyone will have a personal robot, but jobs will be different. A turnip picker in the 1900s lost her job to a tractor and became a tractor driver; "computer" was a job in the 1960s and those computers became programmers. Change is not to be feared.
