Mission 3 // Looking things up

It can ask for help

Your location

“You’re in Pangbourne.”

Web search

“Birch Bread is on Reading Road.”

Opening times

“Birch Bread is open now.”

Wikipedia

“Big Ben’s tower is 96 metres tall.”

It doesn’t know everything, but it knows how to look things up. Then it turns the facts into a story that answers you.

These are called tools. The chatbot can’t see the world by itself, so it uses tools: your phone’s location, a web search, opening times, or an encyclopedia like Wikipedia. Ask the group: what question would need a tool? (“What’s the weather tomorrow?” needs a weather tool. “What’s 2 + 2?” doesn’t.) Big Ben is actually the bell — the tower is called the Elizabeth Tower. That’s a nice bonus fact to check with Wikipedia.
