Final question

Will AI take over the world?

Probably.

Not because of an army of Terminators, but because we hand over more and more jobs to it.

Each handover is a small, sensible choice: the map, the spam filter, the booking, the car. The rule that keeps us safe is the same one as for our agent: it must ask before it does anything important.

Give your honest answer. Probably yes, but not like the films. There will be no moment when Skynet comes online. It happens slowly, as we hand over more responsibility: directions, shopping, banking, driving, medicine, running power grids. Each step makes sense on its own. The danger is not a robot army, it is that we stop checking. So the rules matter: who is in charge, what the AI is allowed to do, and who can switch it off. Click twice for the answer and the reason. Ask: which job would you never hand over?
