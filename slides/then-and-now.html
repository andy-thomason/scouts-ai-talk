Computers keep getting smaller

1960s: a computer filled a whole room.

Now: the same sums, in your pocket.

Photos: computer room with IBM 7090 mainframes, NASA via US National Archives (public domain). iPhone Pro, by Ka Kit Pang via Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Show the 1960s picture first. This is what a computer looked like: a whole room of cabinets, tape drives and people to run them, for far less power than a phone has today. Your phone is millions of times faster and fits in your pocket. Click to reveal the phone: this is what the same thing looks like now. Ask: could the giant AI of today shrink like that? Computers have done it again and again, and AI is already starting to.
