Inference at home: your own AI in a box

“Inference” means running a trained AI to get answers. Today you can do it on your desk, with no data centre.

Nvidia DGX Spark

About the size of a thick book. Runs big models on your desk.

Minisforum MS-S1 Max

A mini PC with 128 GB of shared memory. Also runs big models at home.

Photos: Nvidia DGX Spark, by Daniel Lu (dllu) via Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0). Minisforum MS-S1 Max, product photo from Minisforum

Running an AI to get answers is called inference; training it is the expensive part that needs the data centre. Inference can now happen at home. The Nvidia DGX Spark and the Minisforum MS-S1 Max are small desktop boxes with lots of memory that can run large language models on your own desk, privately, with no internet. They use about as much power as a laptop or a bright light bulb rather than a power station. Ask: what would you do with a private AI that never leaves your house?
