MCP: one plug for every tool

How does the agent talk to the train company, your email and your calendar?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a standard plug. Every tool says what it can do and how to ask, so any AI can use it.

Like USB-C: one socket, many gadgets.

The agent can’t do much on its own: it only produces words. To act, it needs tools: the train booking site, email, the calendar. In the past every tool needed its own special wiring. MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is a standard plug: each tool describes what it can do and how to ask, and any AI can plug in. Click to add the tools one at a time. Link back to rule one: the plug lets the AI reach your email and your money, so it must still ask before it uses them. Ask: which tools would you plug your AI into, and which would you keep unplugged?
