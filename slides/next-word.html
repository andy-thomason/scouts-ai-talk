Part 1 · Guessing

A chatbot just guesses the next word

The numbers are made up, but this is exactly the idea: score every possible word, then pick a likely one.

This is the big secret: a chatbot is a super-powered “guess the next word” machine. It has read a huge amount of text, so it’s very good at guessing what usually comes next. Watch: for “The cat sat on the…” it scores every word it knows. “Mat” is very likely, “banana” is very unlikely. It picks one, adds it on, and goes again. Ask the group: what would YOU guess next?
