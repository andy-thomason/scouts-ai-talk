Part 4 · Agents

What could an agent do for you?

Book a train

Find the times, pick the cheapest, get the ticket.

Send an email

“Tell the group camp starts at 10, and bring wellies.”

Plan a trip

Check the weather, find a campsite, make a kit list.

A good agent always asks before it spends your money or sends something in your name.

Ask the group: what boring job would you hand to an agent? Homework is a fun one to discuss — an agent could find information for you, but if it does all the thinking, you don’t learn anything. And the golden rule: an agent should always check with you before doing anything that costs money or speaks for you.
