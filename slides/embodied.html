Mission 6 // Embodied AI

Giving the machine a body

Same trick as the chatbot, but instead of the next word, it guesses the next move.

ASIMO · Honda, Japan

A pioneer from 2000, one of the first robots to walk like us.

Atlas · Boston Dynamics, USA

Famous for running, jumping and even backflips.

Optimus · Tesla, USA

Being built to do jobs in factories and, one day, homes.

Photos via Wikimedia Commons: ASIMO by Syced (CC0) · Atlas by Damian B Oh (CC BY-SA 4.0) · Optimus by Benjamin Ceci (public domain)

Embodied AI means AI with a body. A robot has to look around with cameras, decide what to do next, move a little, check what happened, and go again — exactly the loop we played with words. Shaping robots like people means they can use our stairs, doors and tools. ASIMO, from Honda, was a famous early walking robot. Atlas, from Boston Dynamics, is known for parkour videos. Optimus, from Tesla, is being designed to work in factories.
