Films love killer robots and alien invaders. This picture is from 1906 — over a hundred years ago people were already scared of giant machines! Click to reveal the punchline. Real robots are much more likely to be stacking shelves, making coffee and folding laundry than marching across the countryside. It’s still right to ask questions: who controls the robot, what is it allowed to do, and who checks it? That’s just like our agent rule — it should always ask before doing anything important.
