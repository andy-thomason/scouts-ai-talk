Mission 6 // Power

A hundred-trillion-parameter AI is hungry

Its brain

200 TB

Just to store 100 trillion numbers. That is thousands of ordinary laptops’ worth of storage.

Its chips

1000s

Thousands of graphics chips working together, each using about as much power as a toaster.

Your brain

20 W

Less than a light bulb. And you can do the whole of this talk on it.

Do we need data centres in the future?

Big AI models live in data centres: huge warehouses full of chips, with their own power stations and cooling. A model with a hundred trillion parameters (numbers) needs about 200 terabytes just to hold its numbers, so it has to be spread over thousands of chips, and that uses the electricity of a small town. Compare it with your brain, which runs on about 20 watts. Click to reveal the question and let them argue it.
