Mission 2 // Attention

Attention is knowing which way to step.

The chatbot pays attention to “capital” and “France”, takes the “capital of” step from France… and lands on Paris.

Recap: words have home addresses, and moving in a certain direction changes the meaning in a certain way — “capital of”, or “baby animal” (dog to puppy, cat to kitten). Attention is the part that looks at all the words in your question, decides which ones matter most, and works out which step to take. The word “the” in the question doesn’t matter much. “Capital” and “France” matter a lot.
