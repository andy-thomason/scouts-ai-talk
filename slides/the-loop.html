Then it does it again… and again

1

Read the story so far

2

Guess the next word

3

Add it to the end

↻

Go back to 1 until the answer is done

Every long answer is built one word at a time.

Psst: the real pieces are often bits of words, called tokens, like “sand” + “wich”.

That’s the whole loop. Even when a chatbot writes a whole essay, it is just doing this over and over, really fast. It never plans the whole answer in one go — just like you in the story game. Bonus fact: the pieces are called tokens, and a long word might be split into two or three of them.
