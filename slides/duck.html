Meet Blue, Nvidia’s duck robot

It learned to walk inside a computer game world.

Blue is a Star Wars-style droid built by Disney, Nvidia and Google DeepMind.

It practised waddling over and over in a pretend world, then walked onto a real stage in 2025.

It shows its feelings with its head and happy little beeps.

Photo: BDX droids like Blue at Disneyland, by Contributor19 via Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 4.0)

At Nvidia’s big GTC conference in March 2025, a little droid called Blue waddled onto the stage next to Nvidia’s boss, Jensen Huang. Blue is one of Disney’s BDX droids, the same kind you can meet at Disney’s Star Wars land. It learned to walk in a simulation: a pretend physics world, like a video game, where it could fall over thousands of times without breaking. Once it had learned, the same brain was put into the real robot. That’s a huge shortcut for teaching robots. Ask: what would you want to practise in a game world before trying it for real?
