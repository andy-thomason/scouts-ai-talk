Part 5 · Making pictures

From fuzz to a cat on a fence

A picture-making AI starts with pure static, like a broken TV.

Step by step it cleans away the noise, guessing what a cat on a fence should look like.

Big shapes come first. Tiny details come last.

Prompt: “a cat on a fence”
Picture-making AIs are a different kind of AI, called diffusion models. They start with random noise — like TV static — and in lots of small steps they remove a bit of noise each time, guided by the words in the prompt. Notice the big blobs of colour arrive first (sky, fence, a dark shape), and the details like ears and eyes come last. Game: “human diffusion”. Everyone stands scattered randomly around the room — that’s the noise. Each time you clap, everyone takes ONE step towards a shape marked on the floor (a smiley face or a cat outline in tape). After about ten claps, the picture appears out of the crowd.
