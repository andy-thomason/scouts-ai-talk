Mission 6 // Embodied AI

Your home robot, 2029

It does the washing-up. It folds your socks. It will be back. (Original drawing, inspired by the films.)

Here’s your home robot of the future. Yes, it looks like a Terminator: in the films, 2029 is the year humans fight the machines. Real home robots are much less scary, but they are coming: companies like 1X, Figure and Tesla are already testing humanoid robots in people’s homes, doing jobs like tidying and loading the dishwasher. Ask: what job would you give your home robot? What would you NEVER let it do? Who should be able to switch it off?
