A chatbot only knows what it read while it was learning — it doesn’t know where you are or what time the shops shut. So a smart chatbot asks for help. It gets your location, searches the web for bakeries, checks the opening times, and only then writes the answer — still one word at a time. Game: three Researcher scouts each hold a fact card (“You’re in Pangbourne”, “Birch Bread is on Reading Road”, “Birch Bread is open now”). A Guesser asks each Researcher a question, then the group builds the answer one word each, using the facts.
