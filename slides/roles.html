Today’s jobs

Everyone gets a part to play

1

Guessers

Pick the next word in the story. (That’s everyone!)

2

Word Finders

Hold a word card and find its home in the room.

3

Attention

Spot which words matter, and which way to step.

4

Researchers

Look things up: where we are, what’s open, Wikipedia.

5

Agents

Actually do the job: book the train, send the email.

Split the group now. Everyone is a Guesser for the first game. Then pick: about a third of the group as Word Finders (they get word cards), one or two as Attention, three as Researchers (they get fact cards), and one or two as Agents. Hand out cards or lanyards with the job names so everyone knows who they are.
