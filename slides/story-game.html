Game time

You are the guesser: one word each
Get everyone in a circle. The prompt is “Once upon a time”. Going round the circle, each scout adds ONE word — that’s exactly what the chatbot does. Rules: one word only, it has to make sense with what came before, and you can’t plan ahead. Round 2: play as a “boring AI” — everyone must pick the most obvious word. Round 3: play as a “creative AI” — pick a surprising word that still makes sense. Real chatbots have a dial for this, called temperature. Ask: which story was better? Which made more sense?
