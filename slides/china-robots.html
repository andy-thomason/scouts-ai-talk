The next wave is coming from China

Unitree G1 · Hangzhou

Small, nimble, and about the price of a car.

AgiBot X2 · Shanghai

A friendly helper that can wave, dance and chat.

Galbot · Beijing

Already running a drinks kiosk in Shenzhen.

In 2025, Beijing held the first half-marathon and the first World Games for humanoid robots.

Photos via Wikimedia Commons: Unitree G1 by Sayanesy (CC0) · AgiBot X2 by JJxFile (CC BY 4.0) · Galbot kiosk by SABAIEE HuangChuis MMADEEG (CC0)

This is the big one. Many of the newest, cheapest humanoid robots are being built in China, and they are improving very fast. Unitree, from Hangzhou, sells small humanoids for roughly the price of a car — far cheaper than robots used to be. AgiBot in Shanghai and Galbot in Beijing are building robots to work in shops, factories and homes; this Galbot robot is serving drinks from a kiosk in Shenzhen. In April 2025 Beijing held the world’s first half-marathon for humanoid robots, running alongside people, and in August 2025 it hosted the first World Humanoid Robot Games, with football, boxing and running. Ask: would you race a robot?
