Mission 4 // Agents

Agents can get things done

Behind the scenes
An agent is a chatbot that can use tools to actually DO things, not just answer. It works step by step: open the website, compare prices, and — really important — stop and check with you before it spends money or sends something in your name. Game: one scout is the customer, one is the Agent. The customer says “Book me a train to London.” The Agent has to say each step out loud as they act it out, and must ask the customer’s permission before “paying”. Everyone else shouts “STOP!” if the Agent forgets to ask. The £24 and the times are made-up examples.
