Mission 2 // Word homes

Every word has a home address
A chatbot can’t understand words the way we do — it only understands numbers. So every word gets a home address, a bit like a house on a street. Words that mean similar things live in the same neighbourhood: puppy lives near dog, but a long way from cheese. Game: give each Word Finder a word card (cat, dog, puppy, apple, pizza, red, blue, happy, sad…). Make each corner of the room a neighbourhood. Everyone has to go and stand where their word belongs, near words that are similar. Then ask a tricky one: where should “hot dog” stand? Between animals and food! Real chatbots give each word hundreds of address numbers, not just two, so a word can be near lots of different neighbourhoods at once.
